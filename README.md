## Exchanges & Regulators

- [HOSE](https://www.hsx.vn/) — Ho Chi Minh Stock Exchange
- [HNX](https://www.hnx.vn/) — Hanoi Stock Exchange
- [UPCOM](https://www.hnx.vn/) — Unlisted Public Company Market (part of HNX)
- [SSC](https://www.ssc.gov.vn/) — State Securities Commission of Vietnam
- [SBV](https://www.sbv.gov.vn/) — State Bank of Vietnam
- [GSO](https://www.gso.gov.vn/) — General Statistics Office
